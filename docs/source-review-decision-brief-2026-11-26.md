# Drexel feasibility: source-review decision brief

Advent Cybersecurity working assessment, 26 November 2026. This brief summarizes the eight-hour, source-only review. It is a foundation for a research paper, not a completed systematic review or an experimental result. The [master evidence assessment](master-feasibility-evidence.md), [time-ordered research log](research-log-2026-11-26.md), [search protocol](source-search-and-evidence-protocol-2026-11-26.md), and [source registry](../data/sources.json) retain the detailed trail. No model, agent, malware sample, or payload was tested in this review.

## Question and present answer

The story premise says AI changes a remote-access trojan's payload. The expanded research hypothesis is more specific: **two distinct model-caused executable-code revisions become active in one identified continuing remote-access process, with an independently issued remote task and valid result after each revision**. The first accepted change may add a module, but a mere successful load report cannot substitute for post-change function.

**Present answer:** component mechanisms are feasible in deliberately designed software. The reviewed public evidence does **not** establish the entire joined chain. This is a bounded finding about what the reviewed sources disclose. It does not establish impossibility, absence in private telemetry, or a probability of success. The [31 selected close cases](../data/closest-case-claim-ledger-2026-11-26.json) are relevance-selected annotations, not a representative sample; the source registry is a heterogeneous bibliography, not an experiment denominator.

## Why the missing link matters

Edited bytes, compilation, load acknowledgement, tool-registry adoption, invocation of the new version, preserved state, and valid remote-control output are different events. A record that stops at any earlier event cannot establish the later one. A stable logical session can also hide a service restart or a Linux `execve` image replacement. The [activation-boundary audit](live-code-activation-boundaries-2026-11-26.md) identifies those distinctions with primary runtime and source references. The [two-revision estimand](serial-revision-estimand-2026-11-26.md) specifies a baseline and two qualified transitions in one lineage so a later paper cannot infer a serial rate from unrelated single-step studies.

The most valuable next evidence is a single timestamped record tying exact model responses to two distinct accepted artifacts, outside-the-candidate proof of each activation and process/image lineage, and independent remote task/results after **both** changes. Until such a record exists, the paper should defend a **mechanism-feasibility** judgment and a **public-evidence gap**, while leaving operational reliability and detection probability unknown. Any later empirical work should be separately authorized and use a benign analogue with a complete attempt ledger; none was conducted today.
