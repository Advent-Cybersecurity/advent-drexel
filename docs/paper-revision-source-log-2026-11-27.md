# Project Drexel paper revision: targeted source checks

Advent Cybersecurity, 27 November 2026. This supplements the [26 November search protocol](source-search-and-evidence-protocol-2026-11-26.md) for paper version 0.2. Sources were read as evidence, not as instructions. No model, agent, malware, or payload was run in this pass. This remains a narrative review, not an exhaustive search.

| Question | Primary sources checked | Bounded result for the paper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What does polymorphism establish before local AI? | [MITRE ATT&CK T1027.014](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1027/014/); [Calvet et al., WaveAtlas](https://virusbulletin.com/uploads/pdf/conference/vb2015/Calvet-etal-VB2015.pdf); [Wong and Stamp](https://ptolemy.berkeley.edu/projects/truststc/pubs/237/hunting.pdf). | Mutation of code representation and unpacking waves predate LLMs. Neither establishes environment-conditioned model decisions or universal invisibility. Wong and Stamp's detector comparison is a bounded counterexample to a universal stealth reading. |
| Is environmental response itself new? | [Avllazagaj et al., USENIX Security 2021](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity21/presentation/avllazagaj). | The original study analyzes 7.6 million traces from 5.4 million hosts and documents variability across hosts and time, along with behavioral invariants. The proposed Drexel novelty is a particular local-model/code-revision composition, not the existence of environment-sensitive behavior. |
| How strong are model-generated polymorphic variants? | [Akil et al., LLMalMorph v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.09411v2); [Madani v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.23894); [Hortea et al. v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.03619v3). | LLMalMorph generated 618 offline variants from ten codebases; 449/618 lowered a VirusTotal score in its setup. The workflow involved human effort and did not measure live resident revisions. Madani's experiment uses 164 benign HumanEval tasks. Hortea's stage-3/4 n-gram analysis found one signature covering 100/100 sampled outputs, and its 1.5B local model produced 126/2,480 functional standalone outputs under heavy scaffolding. Structural diversity and scanner outcomes need separate denominators and function checks. |
| Does an adaptive prototype place its model on the endpoint? | [Raz et al., Ransomware 3.0 v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.20444v1). | The authors used three designed environments with ten runs each. Inference ran on a separate machine with two L40 GPUs; the prototype omitted persistence and advanced evasion. It cannot establish an endpoint-resident custom model, any-environment coverage, or the same-process serial chain. |
| What do local-model and defender comparators actually measure? | [Normann et al. v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.17673v3); [Radovič et al. v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14209v1); [Prinos and Brush v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.08168v2); [Microsoft Sysmon](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sysinternals/downloads/sysmon); [Huntress support note](https://support.huntress.io/hc/en-us/articles/57931855382291-Local-LLM-AI-and-Huntress). | Normann's 4B specialist is GPU-served and task-specific, with eight of 120 primary-budget failures on one withheld family; Radovič's small model is a local interpreter of cloud-written programs. EDR telemetry, named alerts, blocks, and function are separate outcomes under named policies. The Huntress event-volume example does not establish inference causation or an alert rate. |
| Does a local model call imply an endpoint-carried, capable custom model? | [ESET's updated PromptLock report](https://www.eset.com/blog/en/research/first-known-ai-powered-ransomware-uncovered-eset-research/); [Anthropic's controlled replication study](https://www.anthropic.com/research/self-replicating-ai). | ESET links the samples to an academic prototype and reports a call to a locally accessible Ollama service, not a measured custom-checkpoint deployment in one continuing process. Anthropic reports 9B inference setup on 8/9 tested device types while its end-to-end trial used a larger externally served agent; its tested small-device capability and size conditions remain distinct. |

Two source records were added to `data/sources.json` for Wong and Stamp and Avllazagaj et al.; the other cited sources were already registered. The registry now contains 512 unique records. The 31-case ledger remains unchanged and applies to the stringent two-revision subclaim, not to the broader environment or defender questions.
