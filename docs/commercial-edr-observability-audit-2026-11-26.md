# Commercial EDR observability is an experimental variable

26 November 2026. Source-only review; no endpoint, model, or payload was tested here.

[Prinos and Brush, *Closing the Sim-to-Real Gap*, arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.08168v2) evaluates an autonomous **defender** that changes commercial EDR policies while a separate NodeZero attacker runs in an eight-host GOAD lab. Its principal Microsoft Defender XDR campaign has four paired comparisons, 20 pentests, 16 policy-deployment iterations, approximately 2,388 offensive events, and approximately 207,000 defensive alerts. CrowdStrike Falcon and SentinelOne Singularity receive one shorter closed-loop trial each. This is a study of policy attribution and measurement, **not** a model-authored payload revision or a Drexel endpoint pass rate.

The paper identifies three concrete reasons an endpoint verdict needs more than an alert count:

1. Defender XDR's ASR events can be delayed and throttled per process; the authors report a median eight-to-24-minute lag for the observed ASR blocks. Of 15 attacker-side confirmed interventions, 13 had a matching named block within a five-minute same-host window and two did not appear in the queried `DeviceEvents` categories. A missing named block in one stream therefore need not imply no intervention.
2. Different products expose different attribution units. The study tied some Defender and CrowdStrike events to actions using process lineage, while SentinelOne grouped mitigations by threat, preventing the authors from assigning all such mitigations to an individual action. A count of raw telemetry, named detections, and blocks represents three different outcomes.
3. Vendor-managed autonomous response changed between and within runs. With Defender automatic attack disruption left on, the authors had to clear accumulated state; three no-agent baseline runs after reset still yielded different block totals (121, 60, 106). They explicitly decline to attribute cross-run variation solely to the tested policy agent. They also report a mid-campaign change in reset procedure, limiting direct comparison among paired trials.

The paper's appendix defines two different “silent bypass” rates over *admissible NodeZero actions*: unblocked actions versus actions neither blocked nor given a named detection. Raw telemetry is excluded from both definitions. Those action-level rates cannot be read as the chance that a Drexel process remains unnoticed, especially where a complete multistep task or two version-bound checks are the unit of interest. The authors' single lab, single pentester, changing cloud behavior, limited vendor probes, and varying run order further bound transfer.

A [25 November Huntress EDR support note](https://support.huntress.io/hc/en-us/articles/57931855382291-Local-LLM-AI-and-Huntress) supplies a different, production-observed signal: Huntress says it has seen one **local AI session** generate more than 90,000 process events in a minute, and some agentic workloads can raise the EDR agent's CPU/RAM usage. The note gives no cohort denominator, process-event definition, model-placement proof, malicious workload, or alert/block outcome. Its title says “Local LLM AI,” but its described environment is AI coding agents or other local agentic workloads; it does not show that an LLM's *inference* caused the event burst. The observation supports measuring both monitored workload events and monitoring overhead, not a claim that model loading itself triggers detection or that every local agent produces that volume.

**Implication for a future authorized benign Drexel analogue:** prespecify the endpoint product/version, policy modes, autonomous-response state, telemetry surfaces, event-correlation rules, lag window, and outcome unit. Record application-side version and function results independently of EDR data. Report raw events, named alerts, enforcement, retained function, and EDR-agent resource overhead separately; reset or explicitly model carried-over EDR state between trials. A no-alert statement should name the queried surfaces and observation window rather than imply total invisibility.
